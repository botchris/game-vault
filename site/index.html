Game Vault

A self-hosted web app for every game you own: store libraries, CD keys and the discs on your shelves, in one catalog on your own server.

Install with Docker

What's inside

Every game once, with every platform you have it on, every key you haven't redeemed and every disc you've shelved.

The app with a demo catalog. Hover a slice to open it.

How it works

Ask before you buy

Type a game and Game Vault answers with every copy you have: store libraries, keys and discs.

Try it here, on a demo catalog of twelve games.

Ten stores, read only

Steam, Epic, GOG, Battle.net, EA app, Ubisoft Connect, Xbox, PlayStation, Amazon Games and itch.io come in by themselves.

It never buys, redeems or changes anything.

Discs by barcode

Scan box after box with your phone: 118 systems, from the Atari 2600 to the Switch 2.

Each copy keeps its region, condition, edition, shelf and price.

No key dies forgotten

Humble Bundle keys arrive with their redeem dates. The ones about to expire, and the ones you don't need, are flagged.

Deals on what you don't own

With a free IsThereAnyDeal key, the home page shows today's deals and historical lows, never for games you already have.

The shelves count too

Consoles, handhelds and classic computers of every generation, with the details collectors care about.

One container on your server: docker run -p 8080:8080 -v game-vault-data:/config botchrishub/game-vault, then open port 8080. Install

Fair questions

How does it read my stores?

Most stores have no official way to list what you own, so it signs in the way their own apps or open-source launchers do, and only reads. Fanatical forbids that in its terms, so it stays off until you accept the risk.

Is my data safe?

It never leaves your server. Store sessions sit unencrypted in its database: keep the config volume private, and use HTTPS if you reach it from outside. SECURITY.md has the details.

What does it cost?

Nothing for personal use. The code is open to read and change under the PolyForm Noncommercial license; selling it or running it as a paid service isn't allowed.

Can I bring my spreadsheet?

Yes. Import a CSV with titles, platforms, keys and discs, and export everything back the same way.

Was it built with AI?

Yes, with a lot of help from Claude Code. The architecture, the specs and the reviews are its maintainer's, a software engineer with twenty years of experience, and every change goes through tests.
